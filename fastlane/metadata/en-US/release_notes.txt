The trace of the wind ACTUALLY MEASURED now appears on the curve, beneath the forecast: speed and gusts, exactly as the anemometer recorded them. The gap between what was announced and what actually blew reads at a glance, day after day - without having to take our word for it.

Where a measurement is missing, the line breaks and the dots stay visible. A silence of several hours will never be filled in with an invented line.

Custom spots:
- A spot you pinned yourself now takes the weather of the nearest verified location, within 8 km. A pin dropped a few kilometres offshore was reading open-water wind, and could put a sheltered lagoon ahead of an ocean beach. Beyond 8 km your pin is kept: the weather of a distant town would describe another regime.

Ports:
- Andernos-les-Bains (Le Betey), Hourtin-Plage and Lacanau Ocean join the catalogue.
- "Lacanau" becomes "Lacanau (large)": this tide reference point sits 29 km offshore. Its tides are correct, its wind is not the beach's.

A day with no slot now tells you WHY: "no window - max 8 knots". Two spots can be compared at a glance, without opening anything. Silence should never look like a breakdown.

Apple Watch - the watch finally follows the same rules as the phone:
- Real wind can no longer come from a station 30 km away. The radius is down to 15 km, matching the iPhone: a confirmation from somewhere else confirms nothing. And the wrist is exactly where it matters most, on the beach with no phone.
- When a station does not publish its direction, the arrow no longer defaults to due north. It disappears, and the watch falls back to the phone's wind instead of inventing a heading.
- Tide labels and the age of the reading now appear in your language. They had stayed in French everywhere else.

On iPhone:
- The figures in the weather ribbon stay readable over the brightest bands.
- Smoother spot switching: the forecast calculation no longer holds up the display.

Fair winds and good waves!
